Privacy

Last updated 16 November 2026

The short version
  • Looksie has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. It does not send your code, or anything about you, to us.
  • It runs on your computer and talks to GitHub as you, to read pull requests and, when you press Submit, to post your review.
  • If you buy Pro, our payment partner Polar handles the payment and sends you your licence key.

Who we are

Looksie is made by Marcus Smallman-Lewis, trading as Looksie, in the United Kingdom. For anything about your data, write to support@looksie.dev.

What stays on your computer

Everything Looksie works with stays on the computer it is installed on:

  • Sign-ins. Looksie uses the GitHub CLI's own sign-in, or a token you add, which is kept in your computer's keychain (Windows Credential Manager or the macOS Keychain). It is never written to a file and never sent to us.
  • Your reviews. Answers, notes and draft comments, and copies of reviews it has prepared, are kept in Looksie's folders on your computer so you can pick up where you left off.
  • Your licence. Your licence key and plan are kept in Looksie's settings folder.

Uninstalling Looksie and deleting its folders removes all of it. The folders are named dev.looksie.app, in your user's application data on Windows and in ~/Library/Application Support and ~/Library/Caches on a Mac.

What leaves your computer, and where it goes

  • GitHub (or your company's GitHub server). Looksie reads the pull requests you open, and posts a review only when you press Submit and confirm. GitHub's own privacy policy covers what it does with those requests.
  • AI, only if you set it up. With your own Anthropic or OpenAI API key, Looksie sends a prompt made from the pull request (its description and the changes for the decisions) straight to that company, under your own agreement with them. Each key is kept in your computer's keychain and sent only to its own company. Requests to OpenAI ask it not to store the response for later retrieval; each company's own policies cover what they keep. If you connect your own Claude Code or Codex instead, your agent asks Looksie for the same prompt and sends it to its own provider under your account; Looksie only stores the answer on your computer. You can read the prompt in the app first. Without either, nothing goes to any AI service.
  • Update checks. When it opens, and every few hours while it is open, Looksie asks GitHub whether a newer version exists, by reading a public file from our releases page. Nothing about you is sent, though GitHub sees the request come from your internet address, as with any web page.
  • Launch offer. On the plan screen, Looksie reads how many launch-offer places are left from looksie.dev. Nothing about you is sent.
  • Licence checks (Pro only). About once a week, Looksie checks your licence key with Polar, our payment partner, so refunds and cancellations take effect. When you first paste the key, it also sends the computer's name and system (for example "MARCUSPC (Windows)"), so you can tell your devices apart in Polar's portal; a key works on up to three.

When you buy Pro

Polar (polar.sh) is the merchant of record: it takes the payment, handles tax and invoices, and emails your licence key. Polar's privacy policy covers the details you give it. We can see the name, email address and order details of our customers in Polar, and use them only to provide Pro, to answer your questions and to handle refunds. The legal basis is the contract between us. We keep them for as long as the law requires for accounting, usually six years.

This website

This site uses no cookies and no analytics. Its fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which sees your internet address when it serves them. The site's host keeps standard server logs.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. In practice the only thing we can hold is your purchase record in Polar. Write to support@looksie.dev. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

Looksie is a tool for software developers and is not meant for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be here with a new date. If a change affects what leaves your computer, the app's release notes will say so.
